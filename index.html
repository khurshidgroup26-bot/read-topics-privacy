Privacy Policy for Read Topics

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Read Topics" mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built this App to be as privacy-friendly as possible: it does not require you to create an account, and it does not collect or sell your personal information.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information. We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Content You View

The App displays topics, sub-topics, and details (text and images) that are stored in our Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database. This content is publicly readable and is the same for every user of the App; viewing it does not identify you to us in any way.

3. Offline Downloads (Local Storage)

If you choose to download a topic for offline reading, the App saves that content (text and images) directly on your device's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never uploaded anywhere, and is only accessible to you. You can delete any downloaded topic at any time from within the App, which permanently removes it from your device.

4. Advertisements

The App may display banner or full-screen advertisements. These ads are supplied entirely by us (the App's own developer) from our own storage — they are not served by Google AdMob or any third-party advertising network, and they do not track you, profile you, or collect any personal data. If an ad is tapped, it may open a link to an external website in your device's browser; we are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of any external site you choose to visit.

5. Internet Permission

The App requires internet access solely to load topic content from our database and, if used, to download images for offline reading and to open advertisement links. No other data is transmitted from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since no account or personal data is required to use the App, it is safe for use by a general audience.

7. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data of yours stored on our servers to secure. Content you download for offline use is protected by your device's own operating system security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

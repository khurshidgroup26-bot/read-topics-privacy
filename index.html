Privacy Policy

App: Read Topics  |  Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Read Topics" mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App is developed and maintained by Muhammad Ihsan Ilahi Khursheed.

1. Overview

Read Topics lets you browse educational/informational topics and download them for offline reading. The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use it.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect, request, or store:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Login credentials or account information (no sign-in is required)
  • Location data
  • Payment information
  • Contacts, photos, or files from your device (other than what the App itself saves, described below)

3. Content Data (Firebase Cloud Firestore)

The App reads publicly available topic content (text and images) from a Firebase Cloud Firestore database so it can be displayed to you. This content is provided by the App's administrator and is not linked to your personal identity.

4. Data Stored On Your Device

When you choose to download a topic for offline reading, the App saves the following locally on your device only:

  • The topic's text content
  • Any images associated with that topic
  • A local index of what you've downloaded, so it can be shown in "My Downloads"

This information stays on your device. We do not have access to it, and it is not uploaded anywhere. You can delete any downloaded topic at any time from within the App, which removes it from your device.

5. Permissions Used

The App may request access to device storage solely to save downloaded topics (text and images) for offline use. This permission is used only for that purpose.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) to deliver content. Firebase may process standard technical data (such as your device's IP address) as part of providing this infrastructure, in accordance with Google's own privacy practices. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The App does not use advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or any other third-party services beyond Firebase.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no account creation or personal data submission is required, the App can be used without providing any personal details.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties, because we do not collect personal data in the first place.

9. Data Security

Content downloaded for offline use is stored within the App's private storage area on your device, in line with standard Android app sandboxing protections.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
